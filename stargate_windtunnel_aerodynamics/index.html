ASP-9 AERODYNAMIC OPERATING PROTOCOL

1. VARIABLE-PITCH GUIDE VANE DIALING

To establish a gateway inversion through the test section nozzle, steer the azimuth angle around the 12-sector guide vane ring. Aligning a sector commands pneumatic micro-actuators to pivot the straightener vane to its calibrated flow angle and balances pitot-static reference pressure to 0.00 kPa, converting turbulent separation eddies into coherent laminar streamlines.

2. NEVER AUTO-FIRE SAFETY GUARANTEE

Locking all 6 sectors arms the stagnation plenum into PLENUM ARMED / READY TO FIRE. The blowdown sequence will never automatically fire upon the 6th lock. Flow initiation is strictly triggered by manual operator command on the INITIATE TRANSONIC RUN control.

3. THREE-STAGE TRANSONIC ACTIVATION

Upon initiation, the console executes a genuine 3-stage aerodynamic sequence:

  • Stage 1: Buildup Phase (2.2s): Compressor turbine spool-up, Mach ramps 0.20 to 0.95, rising pneumatic whine, and pressure condensation.
  • Stage 2: Breakthrough Instant (0.5s): Transonic Mach 1.0 crossing, optical Prandtl-Glauert Schlieren inversion flash, and sonic boom acoustic impulse.
  • Stage 3: Sustained Active State: Steady-state supersonic flow at Mach 1.45, diamond shock wave lattice in the diffuser, deep resonant roar, and stable aerodynamic balance trim.

4. EMERGENCY BLOWDOWN & DISENGAGE

The hazard-striped EMERGENCY BLOWDOWN DUMP switch is permanently active and reachable in all states. It vents plenum stagnation pressure and returns all guide vanes to neutral.

5. CHOKE GATE SAFETY INTERLOCK

Defaults to RELEASED. When engaged, the physical choke gate blocks nozzle expansion, immediately locking out activation with high-visibility hazard banners and acoustic sirens.
