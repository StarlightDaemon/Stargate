    Operator’s Primer

    Heliora is a threshold annulus — a ring of thirty heliographic sigils that, when attuned in the correct order, opens a standing well of travel-light called the Wellglass.

    • Dial a cant — select seven sigils by clicking them on the ring, or choose a known cant from the Codex. Each locked sigil ignites one of the seven Annular Seals.
    • The Standing Sun — every cant must close with Solyn, the origin sigil (marked in gold at the ring's crown side). The annulus refuses repeats and a premature origin.
    • Sunstrike — when the seventh seal locks, the well strikes open. The aperture holds for thirty-eight seconds.
    • Seal the threshold — the Umbral Occulter, a dark eclipse-disc, transits across the well and quenches it. Let the stability lapse instead, and the well collapses on its own — ungently.

    Keys — R drift cant   S seal   Esc release   M sound   H primer

    A fiction. Every destination named here is invented; the instrument reaches nowhere and no one.
