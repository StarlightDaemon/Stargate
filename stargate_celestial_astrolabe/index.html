HYPERION ASTROLABE — OPERATOR REFERENCE MANUAL

MISSION: The Series IX Digital Astrolabe is a precision celestial positioning instrument deployed on deep void vessels where GPS/beacon networks are absent. It determines exact vessel coordinates by calculating the intersection of 7 simultaneous stellar altitude circles.

1. THE RETE & TYMPAN ALIGNMENT MECHANISM

The console features a digital stereographic Rete (rotating star wheel) mounted over a fixed Tympan plate. Selecting any navigational star rotates the Rete until the star's marker aligns with its target altitude circle along the top sighting meridian. The rotational alignment itself locks the sight.

2. CORE NAVIGATION LOOP
STEP 1: Select 7 Navigational Stars from the Sight Selector (Left Wing).
STEP 2: Observe the Rete rotate and lock each sight against its altitude almucantar.
STEP 3: When 7 sights are locked, the system enters PENDING state.
STEP 4: Press "INITIATE STELLAR GATEWAY ACTUATION" (Right Wing) to open the transit conduit.
STEP 5: Press "EMERGENCY DISENGAGE GATEWAY" at any time to collapse the warp field.
3. EPHEMERIS VERIFICATION HOLD (SAFETY INTERLOCK)

The Ephemeris Verification Hold simulates cross-referencing sights against known astronomical tables. When engaged, it blocks gateway actuation to prevent misaligned transit jumps. Defaults to Released.

4. ARCHIVED QUICK-DIAL PRESETS

Replays pre-computed multi-star sightings at high speed. The auto-dialer moves star-by-star with visible Rete rotation, landing in PENDING ready state without auto-firing.
