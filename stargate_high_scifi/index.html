    STANDING ORDER 4 · CONSOLE OPERATION

    L4 Annex duty card. Read it once; the Artifact is patient but the roster is not. Meridian does not store addresses — it solves them. Nothing you do at this console commands the torus. You propose a metric; it decides.

    1 · Choose how you will address it

    Surveyed route. The ROUTE CATALOG lists manifolds the Annex has already surveyed. Press DIAL beside one and the console hands Meridian a known-good nine-glyph signature. This is the normal duty method.

    Manual entry. Use the SIGNATURE COMPOSER when you are working from a paper survey. Tap eight glyphs from the palette into the slots; the ninth is ERIDU, our own local invariant, and it is welded in place — every solution Meridian relaxes has to terminate here or there is nowhere to come back to. Tap a filled slot to strike it and everything after it. CLEAR empties the rail. When all eight are laid, COMPILE METRIC lights.

    2 · Let it relax the solution

    The bands search solution space and confirm nine topological invariants one at a time — an anchor lights per invariant. Expect fifteen to twenty seconds on a COLD SOLVE. Do not read this as a countdown; it is a convergence, and it takes what it takes. ABORT SOLVE stands the bands down at any point without prejudice.

    A route Meridian has solved before is imprinted permanently in the lattice. A catalog entry carrying the RECALL badge triggers LATTICE RECALL instead: one memory sweep, solution streamed back in under four seconds. The lattice holds eight such imprints. Engineering may purge them; the routes remain, only the shortcut is lost.

    3 · Divergence

    A composed signature that matches no surveyed manifold will still be attempted, and will diverge. This is normal, non-damaging, and logged. One catalogued route has been declined by the Artifact on every attempt since recovery. It is left in the catalog because Standing Order 4 says we do not quietly delete things we do not understand. Do not treat a refusal as a fault.

    4 · Aperture standing

    On convergence the rim brightens and the aperture holds open. Where survey allows it, LAUNCH PROBE sends an instrumented package through and streams its telemetry to the STATION LOG. COLLAPSE APERTURE closes the throat cleanly. Leave no aperture standing at end of watch.

    Readouts: lattice temperature, vacuum strain, solution residual and geodesic cache occupancy report in LATTICE TELEMETRY. Everything on this console is local to this page — the Annex has no line out.

    TRANSIT ARTIFACT M-1 · “MERIDIAN”

    Recovered 2081 from a stable halo orbit at Sol–Earth L4. An eleven-metre torus of scale-invariant grey glass; every magnification reveals the same structure. Radiometric lattice dating: 2.1 million years. Builders unknown. The Artifact does not store addresses — it solves them. Given a nine-glyph metric signature, Meridian numerically relaxes a traversable wormhole solution between here and the target manifold, confirming nine topological invariants one by one. It has never been wrong. It has occasionally declined.

    COLD SOLVE — an unsolved route. The bands search solution space; expect 15–20 seconds of relaxation passes.
    LATTICE RECALL — a route Meridian has solved before is imprinted permanently in its lattice. Recall does not search: one memory sweep, and the cached solution streams back in under four seconds.

    This console is a fictional novelty. It runs entirely in your browser: no server, no network calls, no telemetry leaves this page. All destinations, glyphs and coordinates are invented. Sound is synthesized locally and off by default.
