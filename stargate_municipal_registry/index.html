1. PURPOSE & JURISDICTION

The Department of Interzonal Transit & Municipal Conduit Oversight (DITMCO) regulates all spatial transport apertures within municipal boundaries. Operators must process conduit clearance applications in strict compliance with Municipal Code §144.2.

2. CORE DIALING PROCEDURE (8-FIELD ROUTING MANIFEST)

  1. Select 8 Routing Glyphs from Control Cluster 01. Each selection advances the ledger carriage and imprints a certified bureau docket stamp onto the corresponding chevron position.
  2. Chevron Status Sequence:
    • [DORMANT] — Empty docket slot awaiting assignment.
    • [SEEKING] — Active registry ledger indexing and validation scan.
    • [FILED] — Official bureau stamp affixed; routing coordinate locked.
  3. Review Authorization: Once all 8 fields are verified, Cluster 02 will unlock the [SUBMIT CLEARANCE & ENGAGE] actuator.
  4. Aperture Connection: Press Engage to transmit the manifest. The CRT vector oscilloscope will initialize the municipal transit plane.

3. DISENGAGE & DOCKET RESET

The [REVOKE PERMIT / DISENGAGE] control is permanently active. Actuating this control immediately revokes transit clearance, discharges the conduit carrier wave, stamps the docket [SESSION VOIDED], and resets the terminal to DORMANT for a fresh filing.

4. ADMINISTRATIVE COMPLIANCE FREEZE (LOCKOUT CONTROL)

Under Municipal Rule 9-B / Section 44, municipal auditors may engage the Compliance Freeze toggle. When active, all conduit transmissions are unconditionally blocked. Attempting to engage an active route during a compliance freeze will generate a statutory rejection stamp and sounding alert.

5. PRELOADED QUICK-DIAL DIRECTORY

  • Tier II (Flagged / Audited Corridors): High-risk or quarantine transit lines subject to frequent auditor scrutiny.
