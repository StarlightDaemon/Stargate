BUREAU OPERATING DIRECTIVE // DOC-08

OPERATOR'S APPARATUS MANUAL

I. THE BAUHAUS GEOMETRIC RING

The primary apparatus is an 8-sector Euclidean circle divided along strict 45° orthogonal and diagonal axes. Each sector maps to a primary geometric glyph representing fundamental physical vector transformations (orthogonal axes, equilateral prisms, kinetic arcs, and annular cores).

II. SCREEN-PRINT SQUEEGEE LOCKING

Address registration simulates the physical craft of screen-printing: when a sector is selected, a registration stencil aligns with a sharp mechanical detent, followed by a squeegee sweep that pulls saturated primary pigment across the screen mesh, stamping the glyph into the linear coordinate register.

III. THREE-STAGE STAGED ACTIVATION

Entering 6 glyphs arms the apparatus into a PENDING state. The portal never auto-fires. Activation requires manual dispatch of the primary ENGAGE VECTOR control, executing a 3-stage mechanical ignition:

  • Stage 1 (Buildup - 1.8s): Radial spokes align under mounting metronomic tension.
  • Stage 2 (Breakthrough - 0.6s): A high-velocity geometric vector spearhead pierces the central iris blades.
  • Stage 3 (Sustained Active): Interlocking counter-rotating primary color discs maintain steady harmonic vortex transit.

IV. QUICK-DIAL HARMONIC PRESETS

Preloaded transit routes are organized into two operational tiers: Intra-Metropolitan Arteries (local transit) and Deep Kinetic Corridors (high-velocity long-range vectors). Selecting a preset executes a staged auto-dial sequence (~320ms per symbol), halting in the pending state for operator confirmation.

V. SAFETY INTERLOCK DETENT

The Vector Interference Interlock defaults to RELEASED. When toggled to ENGAGED, attempts to activate trigger an immediate hazard lockout and audible warning tone.
