Codex of Ways

The Grimoire of the Ring

Of the Ring

The Sidereum is a gate-ring of the Astrolith Order, who wrote their magic in the grammar of constellations. Twenty-one star-sigils are inlaid upon its turning band; seven wards stand about its rim in heptagonal array.

Of Inscription

Choose seven sigils, one by one. At each choice the band turns until the chosen sigil stands beneath the Crown Lens, and a ward takes fire. No sigil may be graven twice in one Way. To unmake a half-written inscription, Efface it.

Of the Keystone

When seven wards burn, the Keystone at the Ring's heart wakes. Touch it, and the Way opens. A Way matching the Codex leads to a charted realm; seven true sigils in an order no chart records will still open — an uncharted Way, at the dialer's peril.

Of Swift Working

Graven about the Ring's own rim stands its law: “What the Ring remembers, the Ring may recall.” A Way opened once in full ritual leaves its echo in the stone. To recall such a Way, the band need not turn at all — the wards remember their sigils unbidden and take fire in moments. This is the Swift Working, and it is only possible for Ways the Ring has already known.

Of Sealing

An open Way is closed by the Sealing — the wards release their sigils in reverse order and the veil falls dark. An unsealed gate is an unlatched door; the Order counsels against leaving one open.

Of the Voces Aetheris

The Ring may speak — hums, chimes, and the choir of an open Way — if you grant it voice with the toggle in the ledger.
