    OPERATOR REFERENCE · HELIORA

    Writing a cant by hand

    Click sigils on the band in the order you want them written. Each accepted sigil sends the band seeking under the Meridian Claw, alternating direction sigil to sigil, and seats the next Annular Seal (I through VII) when it settles. The annulus enforces the same rules whether you dial by hand or by Codex: no sigil may repeat within a cant, and Solyn, the origin sigil, may only take the seventh seat — offer it early and the ring refuses it outright. A cant is not attuned until Solyn closes it.

    Codex & drift cants

    Selecting an entry from the CODEX OF KNOWN CANTS auto-dials its seven sigils in sequence, seal by seal, the same way a hand-dial would. DRIFT CANT (R) composes a fresh, previously-unwritten cant on the spot — six random waypoints closed by Solyn — then dials it through the same rotate-and-lock sequence. Nothing about the instrument's motion is ever skipped for a shortcut.

    Sunstrike & the Wellglass

    When the seventh seal locks, the annulus draws breath and then strikes: the Wellglass opens in a burst of molten light. The open well is not stable indefinitely — APERTURE STABILITY counts down from thirty-eight seconds. Inside the last ten, the status lamp shifts to danger and the well itself begins to fray; let the count reach zero and the aperture tears itself shut in an uncontrolled collapse.

    Sealing & releasing

    SEAL THRESHOLD (S) is the proper close: it sends the Umbral Occulter — a dark eclipse-disc — transiting across the open well to quench it cleanly before the seals release. RELEASE (Esc) instead abandons a cant that is still being written, mid-dial, before any strike; it stands the ring down without opening anything.

    Other controls

    The speaker glyph (M) mutes the instrument's synthesized ratchets, bells, and drone without touching anything else. The ATTUNEMENT TRANSCRIPT timestamps every seal, strike, and seal-release for the session. One codex entry, Cor-Avenna, is recorded lost — its cant still locks seal for seal, but the strike finds nothing on the far side.

    Operator’s Primer

    Heliora is a threshold annulus — a ring of thirty heliographic sigils that, when attuned in the correct order, opens a standing well of travel-light called the Wellglass.

    • Dial a cant — select seven sigils by clicking them on the ring, or choose a known cant from the Codex. Each locked sigil ignites one of the seven Annular Seals.
    • The Standing Sun — every cant must close with Solyn, the origin sigil (marked in gold at the ring's crown side). The annulus refuses repeats and a premature origin.
    • Sunstrike — when the seventh seal locks, the well strikes open. The aperture holds for thirty-eight seconds.
    • Seal the threshold — the Umbral Occulter, a dark eclipse-disc, transits across the well and quenches it. Let the stability lapse instead, and the well collapses on its own — ungently.
