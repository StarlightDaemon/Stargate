AEROSPACE STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

ORBITAL AIR TRAFFIC CONTROL MANUAL (SGC-ORBIT-04)

1. ORBITAL RADAR ROUTING

The Orbital ATC Console tracks aerospace flight corridors using 12 radial Waypoints (Zulu-01 to Zulu-12 at 30° azimuth intervals). Locking 9 flight strip waypoints authorizes transit.

2. LOCKING & ACTIVATION PROTOCOL

Universal Safety Rule: Locking the 9th waypoint enters a PENDING READY state. The radar console will NEVER auto-fire upon the 9th lock. The flight controller must deliberately click the glowing AUTHORIZE ORBITAL INSERTION control to transmit clearance.

3. FLIGHT ABORT / VECTOR HOLD

The FLIGHT ABORT / VECTOR HOLD control is always reachable, instantly terminating the radar clearance and purging the active flight strip.

4. ORBITAL COLLISION SAFETY HOLD

Defaults to RELEASED. When engaged, inhibits trajectory firing. Attempting to dial or activate while engaged triggers an audible double-beep alarm and warning banner.
