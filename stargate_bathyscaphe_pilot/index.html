DSV-9 ARCHELON PILOTING PROCEDURES

1. Identity & Operating Consortium

You are in command of the DSV-9 ARCHELON, a Class-IV Titanium Bathyscaphe commissioned by the Hadal Exploration & Abyssal Research Consortium (HEARC). Your mission is navigating planned descent profiles down to the 11,800m Abyssal Gateway Rift.

2. Ballast & Trim Neutral Buoyancy Locking Mechanism

Unlike surface vessels or stationary dialing panels, deep bathyscaphes require balancing 10 independent ballast and trim channels (Main Forward/Aft tanks, Variable Buoyancy Cylinders, and Vectored Thrusters) to reach a stable Neutral Buoyancy Hold (ΔF = 0.00 kN) at each depth waypoint before that waypoint is locked into the descent computer.

3. Staged Descent & Aperture Breach

Once all 8 waypoints are stabilized, the system enters the PENDING / ARMED state (it will NEVER auto-fire). The operator must manually engage the COMMENCE DESCENT APERTURE actuator. The descent executes across three deliberate stages:

  • Stage 1 (Buildup): 2.0s descent acceleration, hydraulic pressure surge, hull creak audio.
  • Stage 2 (Breakthrough): Target depth reached at 11,800m, dimensional aperture breach, optical flash.
  • Stage 3 (Sustained Active): Stable hold at depth, live particle vortex, continuous active telemetry.

4. Emergency Disengage & Ascent

The EMERGENCY ASCENT // BLOW ALL BALLAST control remains accessible at all times. Actuating it instantly vents all 10 trim channels, purges the descent profile, and rapidly surfaces the bathyscaphe.
