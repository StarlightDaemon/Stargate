# Pan-Harmonic Bibliographic Depository (`stargate_bibliographic_depository`)

> **Operator Review & Thematic Finding**: Direct operator testing confirmed that the core interactive loop (manual dialing, two-step authority cross-reference locking, three-stage stasis vault activation, disengage, and quick-dial replay) functions correctly and reliably. However, operator review concluded that the bibliographic/archival depository theme does not suit the project's digital-forward, glowing-schematic visual identity as naturally as prior themes (such as signal-search arrays, physics instrumentation, or celestial navigation). The functional mechanics operate as designed, but the thematic and aesthetic fit for this specific visual paradigm is weaker.

---

## Overview

An interactive digital archival retrieval console and deep-storage stasis portal belonging to the **Pan-Harmonic Bibliographic Institute & Depository (PBID-7)**.

Rather than dialing physical destination coordinates, this console locates preserved bibliographic accession records across millions of universal documents, materializing retrieved items through a high-energy stasis aperture.

## Features

- **Valerius Hex-Facet Taxonomic System (VHFS)**: 10 radial domain divisions (0-9) and 7-facet accession coordinate addresses.
- **Digital Rotary Card-Catalog Wheel**: 10-segment radial card wheel rotating under a fixed collimator reading aperture.
- **Two-Step Archival Locking Gesture**: Segment alignment followed by an automated secondary authority concordance check, locking with a mechanical detent click and reference-desk bell chime.
- **Strict Two-Cluster Core Loop**:
  - *Cluster 1: Taxonomic Classification Assembly* (10 domain buttons & dialed accession slots).
  - *Cluster 2: Retrieval Actuator & Interlock Console* (Master retrieval button, Preservation Integrity Hold switch, and Disengage/Seal control).
- **Three-Stage Stasis Vault Activation**: Buildup (2.0s) -> Breakthrough (iris flare) -> Sustained Active (volumetric vortex & floating holographic folio projection).
- **Preservation Integrity Hold**: Archival safety switch defaulting to released, safely blocking retrieval when engaged.
- **Quick-Dial Presets & Rejection Path**: 6+ preloaded historical records across Verified and Provisional tiers, plus a misfiled/corrupted record demonstration triggering discordant rejection buzzer alarms.
- **Secondary Panels**: Searchable Master Catalog (24+ records), Deep Stacks 3D Map, Provenance & Chain-of-Custody Ledger, Settings Panel, and Operator Reference (`?`).
- **Zero-Asset Web Audio Synthesizer**: Procedural audio generation for all tactile clicks, detents, bell chimes, buzzers, and ambient stasis drones.
