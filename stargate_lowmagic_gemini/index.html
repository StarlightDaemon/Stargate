THE HERBWIFE'S HANDBOOK

A Simple Guide to Cottage Wards and Hearth-Dialing

I. Low Magic vs. Grand Arcana

This hearth-stone is not a cathedral of high celestial constellations or glowing technomantic circuitry. It is humble kitchen craft: flagstone salt lines, spun linen cords, dried yarrow, and tallow-dipped rushes. Everything is made by hand from field and hedgerow.

II. How to Dial a Hearth

  • The Spindle: Click any of the 28 folk symbols on the turning ring. The spindle will rotate to the North Hearth Pin and inscribe a cord inward.
  • Tying Seven Knots: Every parish destination requires exactly seven symbols. When all seven are tied, the hearth enters the Bound & Pending state.
  • The Kindler: Press Kindle Hearth [K] to blow the bellows and ignite the rushlight wick. The gate will never open on its own.
  • Snuffing: Press Snuff Hearth [Esc] at any time to sever the cords with sheep shears and smother the coals.

III. The Three Stages of Kindling

  • 1. Buildup (1.8s): The bellows breathe, the tallow wick catches, linen cords hum with rising tension.
  • 2. Breakthrough: The salt line fractures with a sharp chalk snap; the bronze hearth-bell chimes; botanical silhouettes blossom.
  • 3. Sustained Active: The threshold opens. Night moths and thistle seedheads drift lazily through the warm aperture.

IV. The Cold-Iron Threshold Nail

Old folk tradition holds that cold iron bars the fae and prevents hearth-magic from catching. When the Iron Nail is Driven, the Kindler is barred and will clang shut. Click Cold Iron Nail [I] to draw the nail and permit kindling.
