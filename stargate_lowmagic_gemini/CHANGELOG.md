## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-04

**Built by:** Gemini 3.8 Flash (High)

### Added
- Initial release of the Stargate Rushlight Hearth console (`stargate_lowmagic_gemini`).
- Authentic flat illustrative folk-craft register based on illuminated manuscript marginalia, botanical herbarium plates, cross-stitch sampler borders, and chalk/salt hearth sigils.
- Dedicated refusal of both past failure modes: zero simulated physical 3D textures (no fake wood grain, fake brass, or patina) and zero digital HUD/circuitry styling (no cyan/amber circuit glows).
- The Hearth-Stone Spindle dialing instrument with 28 custom folk symbols (herbs, hearthware, cottage charms).
- "Tying the Knot" locking mechanism featuring rotating spindle alignment and inward-drawing linen cord stitches.
- Full destination autocomplete and typeahead registry containing 50 domestic and pastoral destinations (16 seed destinations + 34 newly invented destinations).
- Sequential auto-dialer for preset destinations (~200ms per glyph with visible spindle movement).
- Genuine three-stage activation sequence: Buildup (1.8s), Breakthrough instant with salt fracture and botanical burst, and Sustained Active threshold with floating seedheads and warm candlelit hum.
- Strict negative test compliance: dialing 7 symbols leaves the system pending Kindle; never auto-fires.
- Safe disengage ("Snuffing the Hearth") reachable at all times, with dual timer-cancellation and state verification guarding against orphan loops.
- Cold-Iron Threshold Nail safety interlock (defaults to Released) with visual warning rhyme when engaged.
- Procedural Web Audio engine producing folk acoustic sounds (plucked twine, chalk scrapes, bellows breath, sheep-shear snip, and hearth bell).
- Accessibility support including `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance, live region state captioning, and full keyboard operability.
