SOLARPUNK SMART DISPATCH MANUAL (SGC-SOLAR-03)

1. ECO-GRID ROUTING OVERVIEW

The Solarpunk Eco-Grid Dispatch Console synchronizes 10 microgrid substations stationed at 36° intervals (α through κ). Selecting 8 renewable resource vectors builds the quantum power pathway.

2. LOCKING & ACTIVATION PROTOCOL

Crucial Rule: Synchronizing the 8th vector enters a PENDING READY state. The gate will NEVER auto-fire upon the 8th lock. The grid operator must manually press the glowing ENGAGE BIOSPHERE CONDUIT button to initiate power transit.

3. GRID SCRAM & BUS PURGE

The GRID SCRAM / BUS PURGE button is always reachable and active, instantly de-energizing all 10 substations and returning the system to idle equilibrium.

4. SUPERCONDUCTING BUS OVERLOAD HOLD

Defaults to RELEASED. When toggled ON, power surge is inhibited to protect cryogenic bus lines. Blocked actions trigger visual interlock warning and alarm tones.
