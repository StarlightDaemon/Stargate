BCAD FORM 9–C · WATCH OFFICER’S CARD · INDUCTION DRILL

Station 3 “Bluff Creek”, Continental Threshold Warning Line. You are the watch. The scope in front of you is not a display of the aperture — it is the aperture, seen down the tube. Nothing opens until the Bureau is satisfied it knows what is on the other end. Work the card in order.

1 · Bring the set up

Raise H.T. The scope will not paint on a cold set; the sweep runs but the phosphor takes nothing. Give it the moment it asks for.

2 · Lay the gates on the station

Read the bearing and range of your intended station off the TARGET LOG, then walk the BEARING cursor and the RANGE gate onto it. The grid is coarse — fifteen degrees, twenty-five miles — and the gates must straddle the station exactly. Close enough is not on the card.

3 · Build a firm track

Now wait, and let the sweep do it. Each pass that finds the blip inside both gates paints it once. Three paints and the track is firm — the LOCK legend lights. Move either knob before that and you drop the track and start the count again; that is the interlock working, not a fault.

4 · Challenge it

A firm track is not an identified station. Press CHALLENGE and the interrogator puts the question to it; a valid station returns its four-figure signature and VERIFY lights. One station on the log, CHARLIE GLASS, has been dark since fifty-four. It will paint, it will track, and it will answer NO JOY every time. Log it and move on.

5 · Induct

Lift the cover by its latch and throw INDUCT. The aperture opens in the tube. The cover is not decoration — while it is down it physically shields the switch, and an unverified induct springs the switch straight back. Drop the cover when you are done.

Fast dial — the drum store

Every verified firing solution writes itself to the magnetic drum, four slots deep; slot 1 ships pre-seeded with ABLE ORCHARD from the acceptance trials. RECALL slews the gates by servo and replays the stored signature off the drum, skipping sweep acquisition entirely. An empty slot refuses recall. This is the only sanctioned way to skip the drill.

All stations, signatures and coordinates on this console are fictional. Nothing leaves this page.
