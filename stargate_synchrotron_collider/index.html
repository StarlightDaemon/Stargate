1. MANUAL LATTICE STEERING (CORE CLUSTER 1)

The Aethel-Ring Collider uses 16 superconducting bending sectors (S-01 through S-16). To steer the beam toward a destination, select a sector button, then click ENGAGE SECTOR DIPOLE. A complete trajectory vector requires exactly 6 sector corrections to cancel sextupole resonances and align the beam at Aperture Zero.

2. BEAM COLLISION ACTIVATION (CORE CLUSTER 2)

NEVER AUTO-FIRES: Completing the 6th sector enters the BEAM READY / COLLISION ARMED state. The operator must explicitly press the highlighted TRIGGER BEAM COLLISION button to initiate counter-rotating bunch collision and establish the topological inversion portal.

3. SAFETY QUENCH PROTECTION & BEAM DUMP

Quench Interlock: Defaults to RELEASED. When toggled on, it simulates a cryogenic thermal trip and blocks beam injection with audible/visual alarms.
Beam Dump / Abort: Permanently available at all times to immediately dump beam energy into the absorber blocks and reset the lattice.

4. PRELOADED QUICK-DIAL & REGISTRY

Access calibrated targets across Tier 1 (Validated Leptonic), Tier 2 (Heavy Hadronic), and Tier 3 (Exotic Singularity) via the Target Registry or quick-dial shortcut buttons to instantly load pre-calculated steering vectors into the lattice.
