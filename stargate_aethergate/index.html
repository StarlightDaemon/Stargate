Operator's Manual

  1. Compose an address. Tap seven distinct sigils on the pad, pick a charted destination, or press R for random coordinates.
  2. Engage the ring. The sigil track spins each glyph to the crown marker and a mechanical seal clamps shut for every encoded sigil — seven locks in all.
  3. Aperture opens. When the prime seal engages, stand clear of the vestibule: the forming event horizon surges outward before it settles.
  4. While open, you may launch a telemetry probe. Watch aperture stability — the ring cannot hold a lane forever and will emergency-seal at zero.
  5. Seal the portal when you're done, or press Esc.

Operator Reference — Console 7

Manual dial

Tap seven distinct sigils on the Sigil Pad to fill the Destination Sequence, one glyph per slot, in the order you want them encoded. Press ENGAGE RING — the sigil track spins to each queued glyph under the crown marker, and a mechanical seal clamps shut the moment it settles. Seven route seals lock in turn, then the prime seal arms and engages: the aperture will surge outward before the event horizon settles into an open lane.

Fast dial

RANDOM COORDS (R) drafts seven random sigils in one press. The Charted Destinations list loads a full known sequence with a single click — no pad-tapping required. CLEAR (C) wipes the buffer while the ring is idle.

Lane open

Aperture stability starts draining the instant the horizon forms — watch the meter beneath the ring. Under 40% wrap up your business; under 15% expect an automatic emergency seal. LAUNCH PROBE sends a telemetry drone through the horizon for seeded survey readings. SEAL PORTAL (Esc) closes the lane on your own terms.

Abort

While dialing, ABORT (Esc) halts the sequence, releases any seals already clamped, and spins the track down before anything locks.
