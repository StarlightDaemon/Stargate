ABYSSAL SONAR COMMAND MANUAL (SGC-NAUT-01)

1. ACOUSTIC DIALING OVERVIEW

The Abyssal Sonar Console routes quantum bathymetric conduits by tuning 8 circular hydrophone transducers stationed along the perimeter (000° to 315°). Locking 6 acoustic frequency signatures prepares the conduit.

2. LOCKING & ACTIVATION PROTOCOL

Crucial Rule: Selecting the 6th signature enters a PENDING READY state. The gate will NEVER auto-fire upon the 6th lock. The operator must deliberately press the illuminated EMIT ABYSSAL RESONANCE control to form the cavitation conduit.

3. DISENGAGE & ABORT CONTROLS

The ABORT / DECAVITATE control is always active and reachable at any point during calibration or active wormhole transit. Pressing it instantly collapses the acoustic conduit and resets the hydrophone array.

4. CAVITATION SAFETY HOLD

Defaulted to RELEASED. When toggled ON, acoustic emission is physically inhibited to prevent transducer cavitation fatigue. Any attempt to engage with the safety on will trigger an audible claxon and visual interlock banner.
