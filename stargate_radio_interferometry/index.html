1. Mission & Instrument Identity

The Hesperus Deep-Space Synthesis Array (HDSSA) is a 9-station radio telescope interferometer operating across the neutral hydrogen and hydroxyl water hole band (1.420 – 1.666 GHz). To eliminate local Radio Frequency Interference (RFI), selecting a candidate sector triggers a 9-station baseline cross-correlation check across Stations M-01 through M-09. Only candidate signals verified across independent baseline stations with phase closure (Δφ < 0.05 rad) achieve sector lock.
