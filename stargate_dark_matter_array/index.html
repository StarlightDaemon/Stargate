1. CORE SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE: BACKGROUND REJECTION

Dark matter particles (WIMPs, Axions) cannot be directly seen. They are inferred solely from extremely rare nuclear recoil (NR) elastic collisions inside the 3.5-tonne ultra-pure liquid xenon active volume, deep beneath 1,450 meters of mountain granite. Most detected events are ambient background noise (cosmic muons, radon daughters, gamma rays) and MUST BE EXCLUDED.

2. S1 / S2 PULSE-SHAPE DISCRIMINATION (PSD)

An interaction produces two signals: (1) S1 prompt scintillation light (178nm) and (2) S2 delayed secondary ionization charge drifting upward to the gas extraction grid. Nuclear recoils have a characteristic log₁₀(S2/S1) ratio between 1.60 and 2.55. Events with higher ionization ratios (> 2.55) are rejected as Electron Recoil (ER) gamma/beta background.
