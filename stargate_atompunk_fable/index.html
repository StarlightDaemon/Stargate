How to dial the threshold

  1. Seat seven isotopes. Press any isotope on the starburst selector. The next Bohr shell hunts for that isotope's resonance, then captures it with a flash. Shells fill from K outward to Q.
  2. Excite. Seven seated shells leave the console ready — nothing opens by itself. Press EXCITE: the shells contract, break through, and the threshold stays open.
  3. Discharge at any time to drop every shell and start over. It is never locked out.
  4. Preset cams seat a stored address at servo pace, shell by shell. One of them has not answered since the ’57 season.
  5. Lead shutter is a safety interlock. It is released by default; engage it and the console refuses to capture or excite until it is released again.
