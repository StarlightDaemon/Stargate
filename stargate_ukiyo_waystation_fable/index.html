Operator's Reference

You keep the press of the Kagerō Road. A passage is a print: seven blocks stamped in registration open the way to one of ten stations.

  1. Name the province. Click any emblem glyph on the Registry Dial. The dial turns it under the pointer and the sumi key-block stamps — the destination is named.
  2. Follow the warden's mark. A vermillion brush-mark shows the next block of that station's address. Stamp all seven impressions; wrong blocks refuse to register.
  3. Break the passage open. A full address only readies the road. Press the square Warden's Seal to gather the ink and break the wave. Draw the knotted Release Cord at any moment to furl everything.
  4. Traveler's tokens hold pre-cut block sets for seven stations: the wardens stamp the address for you, block by block, faster than any hand — but the Seal is still yours to press.
  5. The Barrier Writ closes the pass under an unfavorable omen. While posted, the Seal refuses. The pass stands open by default.
