House Rules (nailed to the beam)

  1. Six way-marks, then Wren’s own, always in that order.
  2. No dialing what the ledger crosses out. She crossed it out for a reason.
  3. Tallow only holds so long. When the wax gutters, the way falls shut on its own.
  4. The iron pin goes back in the moment you’re done. Always.

Chalk, twine, tallow & a wheel off Marsh Pyecombe’s dray. No two dials alike; none guaranteed. — W. A.
