## Overview

Interactive novelty cyanotype blueprint portal modeled as a physical engineering drawing lying on an architectural drafting table:
- **Bureau Identity**: Vance & Sterling Geodetic Survey Bureau (Est. 1888) — *Imperial Cartographic & Sub-Surface Conduit Division*.
- **Plate**: *Plate No. IV-B: Resonant Toroid Apparatus (Azimuth Transit Conduit)*.
- **Physical Materials**: Deep Prussian-blue cyanotype paper with pale-cyan linework, walnut table background, brass compass divider, boxwood scale ruler, red grease pencil, brass drafting tacks, coffee ring stain, and procedural irregular wear.
- **Mechanics**: 9 Geodetic Locking Nodes at 40° intervals, 7-glyph coordinate address length, synthesized Web Audio drafting sounds, Safety Permit Hold toggle, and 6 pre-approved site plans across 2 tiers.

## Core Controls (2 Clusters)
1. **Astrometric Azimuth Dial**: 24-glyph rotatable armature with 0° apex vernier datum, degree readout, and stepping buttons.
2. **Draughtsman's Action Tools**:
   - `SCRIBE & LOCK NODE`: Scribes active glyph to next node with grease-pencil checkmark.
   - `VALIDATE & COMMIT`: Deliberate final activation trigger (enabled only when 7 nodes are locked).
   - `DISENGAGE / EXPUNGE`: Full release of all node clamps and erasure of blueprint scribes.
   - `FIELD PERMIT HOLD`: Safety review embargo stamp preventing aperture activation while engaged.
   - `SITE PLAN ARCHIVES`: Drawer with 6 pre-approved site plans.
