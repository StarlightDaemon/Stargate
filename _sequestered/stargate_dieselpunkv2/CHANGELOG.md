## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-07

### Added
- Caravelle portal cabinet (Belmont Instrument Works, Cabinet No. 9, 1937)
  for the fictional Pan-Continental Travelways.
- Tuning Group cluster: main tuning dial (wavelength in metres), travel
  band selector, resonance shadow meter with jewel indicator, and vernier
  fine tuning.
- Preselector Cam Memory rack — five works-cut presel keys for fast dial
  against the Directory of Termini.
- Line & Portal Group cluster: line power switch, line-volts meter,
  annunciator status plate, and travel engage lever.
- Directory of Termini destination list.
