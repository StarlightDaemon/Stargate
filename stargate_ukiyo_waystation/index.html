一、雲路関所司の概要 (Overview of the Celestial Passes)

The Bureau of Celestial Passes & Mist Registries (雲路関所司) oversees transit along the sixty-nine mystical waystations of the Celestial Mist Highway (浮世雲織道). The Celestial Woodblock Compass Dial (天象木版方位儀) locks the seven required passage seals (通関手形七印) by carving keyblocks and stamping mineral pigment layers in strict alignment.

二、五層摺刻技法 (5-Layer Woodblock Stamping Technique)

Every selected waystation is progressively brought into existence through traditional Japanese woodblock printing layers:

  • 主版 (Omohan - Keyblock): Bold black Sumi ink outlines and boundary ridges.
  • 地摺り (Jizuri - Earth Ground): Ochre, straw, and warm tea-wash mineral pigments.
  • 藍摺り (Aizuri - Prussian Blue): Deep indigo washes for distant mountains and ocean currents.
  • 朱摺り (Shuzuri - Vermillion Accent): Cinnabar stamps for gates, bridges, and lanterns.
  • ぼかし・印鑑 (Bokashi & Inkan): Gradient color feathering and the official red approval seal.

三、開関手続 (Three-Stage Activation Sequence)

When all seven seals are locked, the system enters the PENDING / READY state. It never auto-fires. An operator must deliberately press the vermillion 開関封印 (Activate Barrier) seal to trigger transit:

  1. 墨気集約 (Stage 1: Buildup, 1.8s): Mineral pigments concentrate and press tension accumulates.
  2. 怒涛開関 (Stage 2: Breakthrough): A magnificent Great Cresting Wave aperture tears open with temple bell resonance.
  3. 通関中 (Stage 3: Sustained Active): Continuous circulating woodblock vortex stream maintains transit stability.

四、関所手形結界 (Safety Interlock)

The Imperial Barrier Interlock (関所手形結界) defaults to RELEASED (Pass Open / 通行許可). If an operator engages the barrier latch, all activation attempts are securely blocked with immediate acoustic and visual warning.

五、盤上解散 (Disengage Control)

The 盤上解散 (Disengage) seal remains reachable and active at all times. Operating it clears the locked seals, stops the auto-dial sequence, and closes any open transit aperture.
