    How to tend the ring

    1. Choose seven waymarks from the board. Each one pledges a share of stored sun: the hoop slews the waymark under an open bay, the share flows down its conduit, and the bay's collector opens to hold it.
    2. The route waits. When seven are held the ring pools its shares and rests. Nothing opens on its own.
    3. Open the Span from the hearth when the commons is ready. The pool gathers, breaks into sunlight, and the span stands open until someone folds it.
    4. Fold the Span at any time, in any state. Shares flow back, collectors close, the hoop settles.
    5. Quiet-hours latch. Released by default. While engaged the span will not open, and the ring says so plainly. Release it to carry on.
    6. Routes the commons has walked are re-pledged from the ledger at ledger pace, quicker than by hand because every share is already promised, but each still has to be routed and held.

    Seven bays, fourteen waymarks, one hearth. The ring belongs to everyone who tends it.
