OPERATOR REFERENCE — MERIDIAN RING

Manual dial

Build a seven-sigil address on the SIGIL INPUT MATRIX, or click sigils straight off the ring face — either fills the same ADDRESS REGISTER slots. Press ENGAGE (or Enter) once all seven are set. The inner glyph band rotates each sigil under the apex reader in turn, alternating direction sigil to sigil, and a hold-clamp seats after every acquisition. The eight clamps close in symmetric order, finishing on the bottom keystone — that seat triggers conduit ignition.

Fast dial

Click any entry under REGISTERED DESTINATIONS to load a known-good address in one step, or press RANDOM (R) to compose an unregistered vector — the matrix and register fill instantly, but the ring still runs its full rotate-and-clamp sequence on ENGAGE; nothing skips the physical dial.

Conduit & transit

Once the event horizon settles, click it off-centre to send a probe, or dead centre to transit through. CONDUIT STABILITY ticks down from ignition and auto-seals the ring at zero; SEAL RING (Esc) closes the twelve-plate iris early. Mid-dial, before ignition, ABORT (Esc) releases every seated clamp and stands the ring down.

Other controls

CLEAR empties an unlocked register; the small SND toggle mutes the synthesized servo, clamp, ignition, and horizon audio; the SYSTEM LOG panel timestamps every dial event for the session.

OPERATOR HANDBOOK — MERIDIAN RING

  1. Compose a seven-sigil address on the input matrix, pick a registered destination, or press RANDOM.
  2. Press ENGAGE. The inner ring rotates each sigil under the apex reader; a hold-clamp seats after every acquisition.
  3. When the keystone clamp seats, stand clear of the aperture: conduit ignition produces a violent forward surge.
  4. While the conduit is open, click the event horizon to send a probe. Stability decays; the ring auto-seals at zero.
  5. SEAL RING collapses the conduit and closes the iris. ABORT releases clamps mid-dial.

Keys: Enter engage · Esc abort / seal · R random address
