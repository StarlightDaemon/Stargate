Operator's Manual

  1. Compose an address. Tap seven distinct sigils on the pad, pick a charted destination, or press R for random coordinates.
  2. Engage the ring. The sigil track spins each glyph to the crown marker and a mechanical seal clamps shut for every encoded sigil — seven locks in all.
  3. Aperture opens. When the prime seal engages, stand clear of the vestibule: the forming event horizon surges outward before it settles.
  4. While open, you may launch a telemetry probe. Watch aperture stability — the ring cannot hold a lane forever and will emergency-seal at zero.
  5. Seal the portal when you're done, or press Esc.
