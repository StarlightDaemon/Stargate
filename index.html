Manual Transit Protocol

  1. Consult the Destination Directory (left panel) for your desired coordinate sequence.
  2. Input the precise 4-symbol sequence using the Sequence Input Console (right panel).
  3. Verify the symbols in the center Ring display.
  4. Press the ENGAGE activator to initialize the transit corridor.

Express Transit Tokens

For rapid deployment, utilize the Express Tokens located next to each destination in the Directory. Inserting a token bypasses manual entry and immediately initializes the transit sequence automatically.
