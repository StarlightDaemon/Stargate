    An original fan-tribute interface. Every symbol, address and destination is fictional. Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent or received.

    RING OPERATIONS MANUAL

    1. Compose an address — select six destination glyphs on the input matrix, then the Solyn point-of-origin glyph (marked ◈) to complete the seven-lock sequence. Or pick a verified entry from the Destination Archive.
    2. Initiate dial — the inner ring spins each glyph to the primary lock; nine lugs engage one by one. Unverified coordinates usually fail on the seventh lock.
    3. Conduit open — the breach surge is not survivable; stand clear of the ring. Launch survey probes through the event surface while stability holds.
    4. Disengage — close the conduit manually, or the ring disengages itself when stability is exhausted.
    5. Aperture shield — seal the opening at any time. A sealed ring blocks unscheduled inbound breaches.
