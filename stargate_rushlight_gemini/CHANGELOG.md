## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-04

**Built by:** Gemini 3.8 Flash (High)

### Added
- Brand-new Low Magic portal console (**RUSHLIGHT**) executing a flat illustrated folk-craft visual language.
- Pure 2D illustrative art direction avoiding both simulated physical materials (no fake 3D wood or brass) and sci-fi digital HUD tropes (no glowing cyan circuits).
- 36 hand-crafted folk sigils (hearth runes, dried botanicals, domestic tools, cottage charms) rendered as clean SVG vector iconography.
- 50 pastoral and cottage folk-craft destinations with 7-symbol addresses, folk lore, distance in leagues, and dried herbal pairings.
- Typeahead autocomplete destination search with instant keyboard navigation (Arrow Up/Down, Enter) and live rune preview.
- "Thread the Hearth Knots" (Auto-Dial) visible sequence stepping through rotation, twine drawing, and bone pin seating (~450ms/glyph), halting at pending state without auto-firing.
- Spindle Ring with 7 perimeter Bone Pins, dynamic SVG tensioned thread matrix, and central hearth aperture.
- Genuine 3-stage activation sequence (Buildup, Breakthrough, Sustained Active) triggered solely via dedicated "Strike Rushlight" hearthstone striker.
- "Sweep the Hearth Salt" (Disengage) button always reachable and responsive, with complete cancellation of timers, animations, and audio nodes.
- "Salt-Line Threshold Latch" (Safety Interlock) defaulting to released, with clear visual and audible feedback when engaged.
- Procedural Web Audio API soundscape synthesizing wooden reed clicks, spun wool tension, loom shuttle snaps, flint strikes, bell harmonics, and warm fireplace crackles.
- Full accessibility compliance with keyboard navigation, visible custom quilted focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion` support, and ARIA live announcements.
