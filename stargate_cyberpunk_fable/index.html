          OPERATOR REFERENCE

          NIGHTGLASS is a decorative fiction — a spire of woven light. Nothing here does anything real.

          WEAVE DECK
          Ten resonance glyphs. Choose any seven, in order, to name a destination. Each chosen glyph resolves from static into clarity, a light-thread arcs to the ring, and one shard of the veil dissolves.
          OPEN LINK
          A complete thread always waits, pending. The link opens only when you choose it: a buildup of gathering light, the breakthrough, then the open link held steady.
          CLOSE LINK
          Always within reach, in every state — it unwinds a part-woven thread, halts a re-weaving, or closes an open link.
          DRIFT WARD
          A hand laid on the loom during drift tides. Released by default. While engaged, the link is held closed and the ring says so, unmissably.
          THREADS
          Remembered routes in two tiers — anchored (kept and trusted) and frayed (kept, with reservations). A thread re-weaves its route glyph by glyph at loom speed and then waits, pending, like any other weave.
          ARCHIVE
          Vesper's loom records: nodes, routes, personas, phenomena, fragments — cross-referenced. Records you have read are marked.
          CHART
          The near Lattice as a constellation: nodes and the threads known between them.
          TELEMETRY
          The near sky, measured: carrier, coherence, throughput, drift — each leaning on the others.
          JOURNAL
          A timestamped record of what you actually did this session, plus your weave history (kept across visits).
          SETTINGS
          Themes, display density, motion intensity, and the loom's audio layers. Kept across visits.
