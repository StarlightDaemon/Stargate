BILL OF WORKING — read before use
  1. Open the DAMPER and wait for pressure to enter the working band.
  2. Draw a certified card from the rack — or compose your own: set a value, STRIKE each of the six columns, and take the card from the out-tray.
  3. Throw DRAW. Certified cards run at governor speed; uncertified compositions are proved column by column.
  4. When all six registers drop, throw the COUPLING.
  5. To close the line and clear the engine, pull VENT.
