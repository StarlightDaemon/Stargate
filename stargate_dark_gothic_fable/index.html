The Gate-Book

Corvane Hollow, the gatehouse. The estate is long empty; the mechanism is not.

To open the gate

  1. Choose six wards from the fence at the right. Each press turns the whole fence-ring widdershins — counter-sunwise, the only way it will go — until that ward's finial reaches the keeper's lantern at the top. There it takes flame from the one candle, and a ghost-chain binds it to the last ward caught. The fence will not catch a ward without turning; if the finial already stands at the lantern it goes the full round.
  2. With six wards bound, the bar lifts and the gate stands unbarred — and nothing more happens on its own.
  3. Ring the bell. The bell tolls three times while the fence spins faster and the gate fills with witch-light (the buildup). Then the leaves fling open (the breakthrough), and the gate stands open on the road beyond.
  4. Bar the gate at any moment — mid-turn, unbarred, ringing, or open. The bar drops, every ward goes dark, and the fence rests where it stopped.

The remembered hands

Five keys hang on hooks. Each is a turning the iron has done before; lift one and the fence turns itself along the remembered groove — faster than a living hand, because it does not hesitate, but still one ward at a time, because it still has to bring each finial to the lantern. It lands unbarred, not open. It only remembers from rest.

The salt

A line of salt across the threshold stops everything: the iron will not turn, no remembered hand crosses, the bell does not answer. It is unlaid by default. Lay it and the line burns white until you sweep it away.

One road

One key opens on a parish nobody has come back from. The gate opens. Something knocks back.
