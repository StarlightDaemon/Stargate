OPERATOR'S REFERENCE — ENGINE No. 3

  1. Set the train. In the CARD TRAIN, present eight index cards from the tray, one after another. Each card feeds the reader and locks one value-wheel ring of the Store to the reading line.
  2. Prove & engage. With eight wheels locked the concordance stands PROVED AT REST — the engine never fires of itself. Pull ENGAGE MILL to execute: the mill runs up, the residual wheel is driven to nought, the bell strikes, and the Aperture opens.
  3. Disengage. The DISENGAGE lever is answerable at every moment — dialing, run-up, or open Aperture — and spills steam, clears the Store, and closes the frame.
  4. Folio rack. Previously punched and attested card trains may be run at governor speed from the FOLIO RACK. The reader still feeds every card in order; the engine still waits for your hand at the lever.
  5. Proving Hold. When engaged, the hold bars the mill from executing any train until released. It ships RELEASED.
  6. Bureau folios. ARCHIVE holds the Bureau's prior concordance workings, cross-referenced folio to folio. SECTION shows the engine in elevation. The DAY-BOOK records your session. ADJUSTMENTS trims display, motion, and the sound of the room.
