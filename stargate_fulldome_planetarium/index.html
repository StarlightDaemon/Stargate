VXD-9000 OPERATOR TECHNICAL REFERENCE

1. SYSTEM IDENTITY & INSTRUMENT SPECIFICATIONS

The VXD-9000 Chrono-Celestial Projector is the premier sky-projection instrument of the Mount Tycho Zenith Fulldome Observatory & Public Sky Theatre. Unlike navigational astrolabes designed to locate position via fixed stars, the VXD-9000 is an inverse-astronomical projection engine: it computes, aligns, and physically projects high-fidelity celestial configurations onto the 32-meter titanium-mesh fulldome.

OPTOMECHANICAL CORE Dual-bearing 12-sector starball cage with 10,800 micro-machined fiber star channels.
FULDOMAL FISHEYE Hex-laser RGB solid-state projection with 180° hemispherical collimation.

3. THE 5-VECTOR LOCKING GESTURE

To dial an ephemeris target, the operator selects 5 distinct celestial sectors. Each selection triggers an automated azimuth stepper pulse, aligns the mechanical cage, and locks the sector into the 5-slot Ephemeris Address Buffer with a mechanical focus click and glass resonance tone.

STANDBY / PENDING RULE: Completing the 5th vector lock locks the optical cage into PENDING state. It will never auto-fire without operator command.

4. THREE-STAGE ACTIVATION & EMISSION

  1. Stage 1: BUILDUP (~1.8s) — Xenon arc pre-charge, starball servo acceleration, laser pump spin-up, and rising audio harmonic whine.
  2. Stage 2: BREAKTHROUGH (~0.4s) — Explosive zenith iris shutter release, optical shockwave bloom, and sub-bass resonant glass chime.
  3. Stage 3: SUSTAINED ACTIVE — Real-time rendering of over 10,000 multi-spectral stars, rotating celestial coordinate grid, volumetric nebulae, and laser projection rings.

5. SAFETY INTERLOCK & DISENGAGE

• Safety Interlock: High-flux laser safety circuit defaults to RELEASED / ARMED. If manually engaged, activation is inhibited.
• Disengage: The red ABORT / DOME SHUTTER RESET button is permanently active and callable during any phase to safely return the projector to standby.
